# My Public Representative

## Platform Overview

**My Public Representative** is a transparency and accountability platform that empowers citizens with unprecedented visibility into parliamentary activities through AI-driven analysis and comprehensive data insights.

## Our Mission

We provide citizens with the tools to understand voting patterns, attendance records, debates, and the contextual factors influencing legislative decisions. Our platform bridges the gap between government operations and public awareness, enabling informed citizenship through accessible, data-driven insights.

## What We Offer

### Core Features

- **Comprehensive Parliamentary Data**: Access to complete voting records, debate transcripts, and legislative histories
- **AI-Powered Insights**: Natural language query capabilities allowing users to ask questions about parliamentary activities in plain language
- **Representative Profiles**: Detailed profiles and behavioral analysis of public officials
- **Contextual Information**: Integration of news coverage, social media discourse, and public sentiment to provide context around legislative activities
- **Attendance Tracking**: Participation metrics showing when representatives are present and engaged in parliamentary sessions
- **Voting Pattern Analysis**: Visualizations and analytics showing how representatives vote on various issues
- **Questions & Answers**: Access to parliamentary questions and official responses
- **Sentiment Analysis**: Understanding public and media sentiment around key issues and representatives
- **Statistical Insights**: Data-driven analysis of legislative trends, efficiency, and patterns

### Multi-Country & Multi-Language Support

The platform is designed to serve citizens globally with:
- Support for 195 countries worldwide
- 184 language options for accessibility
- Automatic detection of user location and language preferences
- Localized content and relevant parliamentary data for each jurisdiction

## Our Principles

### Politically Neutral
We present facts without bias or endorsement. Our platform does not favor any political party, ideology, or candidate.

### Data-Driven
All insights are based on official parliamentary records and verified data sources. We prioritize accuracy and factual reporting.

### Transparent
Clear sourcing and methodology for all analysis. Users can always trace information back to its original source.

### Non-Partisan
We provide no ratings, rankings, or subjective judgments. Our goal is to illuminate, not to advocate.

## Technology & Approach

- **AI-Enhanced Analysis**: Leveraging artificial intelligence to make parliamentary data accessible through natural language queries
- **Real-Time Updates**: Continuous synchronization with official parliamentary data sources
- **Secure & Private**: User privacy is protected; no personal data is shared or sold
- **Open Methodology**: Transparent algorithms and analysis methods

## Commitment to Transparency

This platform is designed for transparency and informed citizenship. We do not:
- Host opinions or editorial content
- Endorse candidates or political positions
- Provide a forum for political commentary
- Rate or rank representatives subjectively

Our goal is to illuminate the workings of government through factual, AI-enhanced analysis, enabling citizens to make informed decisions based on objective data.
