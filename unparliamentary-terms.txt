DETECTION METHODOLOGY
==========================================
The AI system uses context-aware natural language processing to:
1. Identify inappropriate language in parliamentary transcripts
2. Classify severity levels based on cultural and parliamentary context
3. Distinguish between Irish colloquialisms and serious breaches of decorum
4. Track trends and patterns across members, debates, and time periods
5. Provide real-time alerts for immediate intervention when required

Cultural Sensitivity Notes:
- Irish colloquialisms are flagged but treated with cultural awareness
- Context is considered (debate topic, emotional intensity, provocation)
- Historical parliamentary precedent influences severity classification
- Educational approach prioritized over punitive measures
